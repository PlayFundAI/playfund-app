How it works

Everything you need to know about PlayFund.

For families
What is PlayFund?
PlayFund is a payment platform for youth sports. It lets your club get paid in full before the season starts, while you choose to pay in full today or spread payments over the season. There's no extra cost to you — the club covers the fee.
Is this safe? Who handles my payment?
All payments are processed by Stripe, the same technology used by Amazon, Lyft, and millions of other businesses. PlayFund never stores your card number or bank details. Your payment information is encrypted and PCI-compliant.
What if I choose the installment plan?
Your payments are split into equal monthly installments. Your club receives the full season dues upfront on Day 1 — PlayFund and its lending partner handle the collection from you over time. You'll receive a reminder email 2 days before each payment is charged.
What if I miss a payment?
If a payment fails, we'll notify you by email and try again automatically. You can update your card at any time from your confirmation email. Repeated missed payments may affect your athlete's enrollment — contact us as soon as possible if you're having trouble.
Can I change my card or payment date?
Yes. You can update your card on file any time before your next payment date. To change a payment date, contact hello@playfundai.com — we'll do our best to accommodate. Changes must be requested at least 48 hours before the scheduled charge.
Do I need to create an account?
No. You don't need to download an app or create a PlayFund account. Your registration link is unique to your athlete. A receipt and payment schedule are emailed to you automatically after you register.
For clubs
How does the club advance work?
Once your roster is locked, PlayFund's lending partner disburses the full season dues (less the 7.5% service fee) directly to your bank account — typically within 14 days of your roster lock date. Your club gets paid in full regardless of how families choose to pay.
What if a family doesn't pay?
That's PlayFund's problem, not yours. The advance to your club is non-recourse — if a family defaults on their installments, you keep the money. PlayFund and its lending partner absorb the default risk. You never have to chase a family for payment again.
What does it cost the club?
PlayFund charges a flat 7.5% service fee on total season dues, deducted from the advance before it lands in your account. There are no setup fees, monthly fees, or hidden costs. Families pay no extra fees.
What do I need to get started?
Your club's EIN, a bank account to receive the advance, and your athlete roster with parent emails. The bank connection is handled through Stripe — it takes about 5 minutes and PlayFund never sees your account credentials.
About PlayFund
Who built PlayFund?
PlayFund was founded by Jackson Watkins and Clyde-Blaise Niba. Jackson played college basketball at UNC Chapel Hill and has seen firsthand how dues collection stress affects youth sports programs. PlayFund is based in Richmond, Virginia.
How do I contact PlayFund?
Email us at hello@playfundai.com — we typically respond within a few hours. For club partnerships, reach Jackson directly at jackson@playfundai.com.
