Get 50% of your upcoming season covered.

PlayFund lets families spread youth sports fees over time instead of paying the season up front. If your club starts using us because you introduced them, we’ll cover half of one athlete’s season fees.

Only the first parent to ask for each club qualifies. Full terms below.

A coach, board member or registrar. We can’t follow up without one.

We’ll contact your club, tell them you asked, and reply to you. Don’t include anything about your child or any payment details. We don’t need them. See our Privacy Policy.

About the offer. If the club you name completes setup and runs a registration through PlayFund, we’ll credit you half of one athlete’s first season dues at that club. Only the first person to tell us about a club qualifies. If someone has already named your club, the offer isn’t available to you, even if the club joins later and even if you asked independently. One per family, and limited to the first clubs in our pilot. If you register more than one athlete, the credit applies to one of them, not to a combined plan. We pay within 30 days of your club’s first completed registration. We’ll either apply it to your own registration or pay you directly, and we’ll tell you which when we confirm you qualify. We may change or withdraw this offer at any time; that won’t affect a request you’ve already sent. This is an offer from PlayFund, not from your club, and your club isn’t obliged to do anything.
