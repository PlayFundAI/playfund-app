Registration is where we start.

Most payment plans see a transaction. We see a season, and everything a family spends getting through it. We began with the payment that hurts most. Here is what we think comes after it.

01

Registration payments

Families pay in full or over time. The club gets the season up front either way, and stops chasing dues in February.

Live today
02

Club capital and scholarship rails

Clubs need money before registration opens, not just during it: facilities, tournament entry, equipment. The same rails could carry scholarship and hardship decisions, which today are made by hand and are close to impossible to audit.

Exploring
03

The sports wallet

A season does not stop at registration. Gear, travel, fuel, hotels, camps and next year's fees are all the same budget. An account built around that, which pays families back on the money they are already spending on the sport.

Where we're headed

Everything past the first column is direction, not something you can sign up for. The card follows the financing. It does not come first.
