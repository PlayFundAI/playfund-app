Registration is the first third of what a season costs.

PlayFund handles that third today. Two ideas for the rest of it.

Exploring

Earn next season back while you pay for this one.

Dues are one line on a much longer bill. Fuel to away games, hotels for tournaments, boots that last four months, an entry fee for every event. None of that spending comes back to anyone.

  • Families earn on the spending a season already forces on them.
  • What they earn goes toward next season’s dues, not a points balance that expires.
  • A family holding part of next year’s registration signs up again.

Exploring

Show a sponsor what their money did.

A local business writes the club a check and hears nothing until next year’s ask. Renewing becomes a favor rather than a decision.

  • Sponsors fund named things: scholarship places, tournament travel, a month of referee fees.
  • Every dollar traced to what it paid for, because PlayFund already settles those payments.
  • The club gets something it can send.
